# Brand Identity: Fauri (فوری)

> **"فوری جواب، مطمئن گاہک"** — Instant Response, Satisfied Customer.
> Intelligent customer message triage for Pakistani online merchants, clinics, academies, and service businesses.

---

## 1. Brand Name & Philosophy

### **Fauri** (Urdu: **فوری**)
- **Phonetics:** `/fɔːriː/` (pronounced *"FAW-ree"*)
- **Syllables:** 2 syllables. Clean, punchy, memorable, and effortlessly pronounced by both English and Urdu speakers.
- **Urdu Etymology & Meaning:** 
  The Urdu word **فوری (Fauri)** means *immediate, prompt, without delay, instantaneous*. In Pakistani commerce, WhatsApp is the default operating system of business. Hundreds of incoming voice notes and mixed Roman Urdu/Urdu messages overwhelm solo founders, clinic receptionists, and store managers. A message delayed by two hours is a lost order or a frustrated patient. **Fauri** promises immediate clarity: every message is instantly understood, triaged, and surfaced by urgency.

### Rejected Alternatives
1. **Pehchaan (پہچان)** — *Meaning: "Identity / Recognition"*
   - *Reason for Rejection:* Pehchaan conveys identity cards (CNIC), KYC compliance, or biometric verification. While 2 syllables, it feels bureaucratic rather than dynamic and fast-paced. It lacks the direct emotional promise of rapid customer support resolution.
2. **RabtaAI (رابطہ AI)** — *Meaning: "Connection / Contact AI"*
   - *Reason for Rejection:* "Rabta" is one of the most overused words in Pakistani tech, telecoms, and government directories. Appending "-AI" creates an artificial, derivative brand that sounds like an MVP project rather than an enduring enterprise-grade SaaS.

## 2. Visual Identity & Symbolism

### The Logomark
- **Visual Motif:** A dynamic synthesis of the Urdu letter **ف (Fé)** with a forward-moving communication bubble and an instantaneous spark / lightning apex.
- **Message:** Seamless fusion of local Pakistani linguistic context (Urdu calligraphy curves) with high-performance computational triage.

### Color Palette

| Token Name | Hex Code | Role | Context / Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Deep Primary (Emerald Forest)** | `#06241A` / `#0C3E2E` | Primary Backgrounds & Heavy Text | Evokes trust, security, and prestige. Resonates with Pakistani cultural heritage and digital commerce authority. |
| **Vibrant Primary (Crescent Emerald)** | `#10B981` | Action accents, success, highlights | Modern fintech vibrancy, forward momentum, alive state. |
| **Warm Accent (Amber Gold)** | `#F59E0B` / `#D97706` | Urgency attention, badges, alerts | Warmth, vigilance, prioritizing messages needing immediate action. |

| **Surface Light** | `#F8FAFC` | Light mode canvas | Crisp, clean, distraction-free readability. |
| **Surface Dark** | `#0B1219` | Dark mode canvas | Deep charcoal slate, reduced eye strain during late-night inbox reviews. |
| **Neutral Borders** | `#E2E8F0` / `#1E293B` | Dividers & Cards | Subtle contrast structure. |

## 3. Typography Hierarchy

### Latin Typography: **Plus Jakarta Sans**
- Fallbacks: `Inter`, `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `sans-serif`
- Crisp geometric grotesque with warm friendly curves. Highly legible at 11px-13px for metadata, badges, and dense inbox tables.

### Urdu Typography: **Noto Nastaliq Urdu**
- Provider: Google Fonts (`family=Noto+Nastaliq+Urdu:wght@400;600;700`)
- Standard Pakistani script for native Urdu text. Correct baseline alignment, cursive ligature handling, and RTL bidirectional support.

---

## 4. Design Guidelines & Tone of Voice
- **Tone:** Professional, reliable, empathetic to busy business owners, fiercely efficient.
- **Bilingual Interface:** English for administrative menus with seamless inline Nastaliq script for customer queries, audio transcripts, and summary cards.
- **RTL Awareness:** Automatic direction detection (`dir="rtl"` vs `dir="ltr"`) based on Arabic/Urdu unicode characters in message bodies.
